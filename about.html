About Queens Berry

Queens Berry is a Malaysian wholesale and consignment supplier of everyday hair, travel and essential accessories.

We supply to mamak shops, multi-branch/independent convenience stores, pharmacies and petrol stations across Malaysia. Our consignment model means zero upfront commitment — you only pay for what sells.

Every product is sourced for quality and priced to keep your margins healthy.
